\begin{minipage}{\linewidth}\footnotesize
This table decomposes embedding variance and the forecast's covariance with next open-to-open returns across principal-component bands, ordered by decreasing training eigenvalue, 2004--2025. The first columns give each band's percentage of the total. $\bar\phi$ averages the ridge shrinkage factor $\lambda_k/(\lambda_k+\lambda)$ within the band, where $\lambda_k$ is the $k$th training eigenvalue and $\lambda$ the ridge penalty. Band-share columns give each component's percentage of the band's variance or covariance; PC $k$ contributes its ridge coefficient $w_k$ times the covariance of the component's coordinate with returns. Panel A uses category ($C$), firm ($F$), and idiosyncratic ($I$) components from a two-way projection on the ridge training window, $T-8$ through $T-3$. Panel B separates cells of RavenPack event group by headline direction into group centroids (Group), deviations of cell centroids from them (Direction), and within-cell residuals (Within cell). Variance and shrinkage average test years; covariance pools firm-days after removing day means.
