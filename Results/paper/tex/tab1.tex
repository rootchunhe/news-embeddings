\caption{Return Forecasts from Event Categories and Headline Embeddings}
\label{tab:tab1}
\begin{minipage}{\linewidth}\footnotesize
This table reports out-of-sample forecasts of next open-to-open returns from news classifications and headline embeddings over 2004--2025. Category forecasts are mean returns over the one or eight years before test year $T$, shrunk toward their event group's mean with weight $n/(n+200)$ on the category mean, where $n$ is the category's headline count. Embedding forecasts use ridge regressions on RoBERTa-large and Llama-2 (13B) embeddings, trained on $T-8$ through $T-3$ and validated on $T-2$ and $T-1$. Coarser forecasts are eight-year means by RavenPack event group or RavenPack type, headline direction from a fixed word rule, or group by direction. LMMD is the Loughran--McDonald positive-minus-negative word count divided by the headline's word count. ESS is RavenPack's event sentiment score. Rank IC is the mean daily Spearman correlation with returns, in percent, with Newey--West $t$-statistics (5 lags). SR is the annualized Sharpe ratio of equal-weighted and value-weighted decile long-short portfolios, with value weights from previous-day capitalization. Net SR deducts 10 basis points per 100\% turnover, or 20 below the NYSE 20th capitalization percentile, when $\gamma$ of the gap between drifted holdings and the target portfolio is traded each day.
