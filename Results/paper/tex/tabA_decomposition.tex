\caption{Content and Timing Decomposition of the Embedding Forecast}
\label{tab:tabA_decomposition}
\begin{minipage}{\linewidth}\footnotesize
This table reports the content and timing decomposition of embedding forecasts of next open-to-open returns, 2004--2025. A two-way projection of the headline embeddings of the up to six years before each test year on category and firm indicators gives category ($C$), firm ($F$), and idiosyncratic ($I$) components, and the forecast's ridge coefficients give $\hat y-\bar y=\hat y^C+\hat y^F+\hat y^I$, where $\bar y$ is the ridge intercept. $F$ is the firm effect for firms with at least ten headlines in the projection window, spread over at least two of its calendar years, and zero for other firms. The first block splits the category and idiosyncratic components into historical levels, which average their monthly means over the firm's news months in the preceding 12 months, and deviations; firm identity lies entirely in the level. The second block instead splits the idiosyncratic component into the firm's mean over its headlines of the preceding 20 calendar days, $\hat y^I_H$, and the remainder. Shares divide each component's covariance with returns, or its variance, by the forecast's. Moments pool firm-days after removing day means. Standalone columns use each component as a signal. Rank IC is the mean daily Spearman correlation with next open-to-open returns, in percent. SR is the annualized Sharpe ratio of equal-weighted (EW) and value-weighted (VW) decile long-short portfolios.
