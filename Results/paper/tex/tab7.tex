\caption{Forecast Covariance with Returns across Horizons}
\label{tab:tab7}
\begin{minipage}{\linewidth}\footnotesize
This table reports forecast covariances with returns across horizons for signal years 2004--2025. Panel A uses the daily ridge forecast, Panel B its headline-weighted monthly average, and Panel C a ridge forecast fitted to monthly average embeddings with the daily forecast's training and validation years and penalties selected by monthly rank IC. Daily $h=0$ is the nontradable return to the eligible open and $h\ge1$ the $h$-th subsequent open-to-open return; monthly $h$ counts months after the signal month. The historical level is the mean of the firm's monthly average forecast over its news months in the preceding 12 months, computed with the current coefficients; without such months it is the forecast from the firm effect of a two-way category--firm projection of headline embeddings, or zero when the firm has $F=0$. The deviation is the remainder. Cov is the cross-sectional covariance times $10^6$, pooled across firm-days or firm-months; Share is the percentage of the forecast's covariance, omitted when the latter has $|t|<2$. Newey--West $t$-statistics use 5 daily or 6 monthly lags.
