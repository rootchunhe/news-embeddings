\begin{minipage}{\linewidth}\footnotesize
This table reports how well earnings headlines explain earnings surprises and predict next-day returns, 2008--2025. Headlines arrive between a timed IBES announcement and 09:00 New York time on its assigned trading day. SUE is actual earnings minus consensus, divided by the previous day's price, winsorized at the 1st and 99th percentiles and expressed as a percentile rank among the announcements of the preceding 12 months. $K$ denotes category dummies, $\hat y$ the CKX forecast, and $E$ the headline embedding. $E_L$ is the firm's historical level, the mean of its monthly average embeddings over its news months in the preceding 12 months, and $E_D=E-E_L$; in $K+E_D+E_L$ they have separate coefficients and ridge penalties. All specifications use the same headlines; coefficients use all earlier years, and ridge penalties are chosen on the preceding year. Panel A reports out-of-sample $R^2$ relative to the training mean and Spearman correlations, averaged over test years. Panel B's $R^2$ is relative to a zero forecast, and its rank IC is the mean daily Spearman correlation with next open-to-open returns, in percent.
