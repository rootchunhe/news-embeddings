\begin{minipage}{\linewidth}\footnotesize
This table reports annualized Sharpe ratios before and after trading costs of daily equal- and value-weighted decile long-short portfolios held from open to open, 2004--2025. Signals are the one- and eight-year category means, the embedding ridge forecasts, RavenPack's event sentiment score (ESS), Loughran--McDonald sentiment (LMMD), and eight-year means by RavenPack event group, RavenPack type, headline direction from a fixed word rule, or group by direction. Rebalancing follows $w_t=(1-\gamma)\tilde w_{t-1}+\gamma w_t^*$, where $\tilde w_{t-1}$ is the drifted holding and $w_t^*$ the target. Costs per 100\% turnover are 10 basis points for stocks at or above the NYSE 20th capitalization percentile and 20 basis points below it. Turnover is half the sum of absolute changes in the long-short weights; holdings drift with each stock's return relative to the long-short return.
