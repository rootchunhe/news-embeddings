\begin{minipage}{\linewidth}\footnotesize
This table reports the contribution of embeddings beyond event-category forecasts of next open-to-open returns over 2004--2025. Ridge estimation for test year $T$ uses $T-8$ through $T-3$ and validation $T-2$ and $T-1$. Panel A reports daily Fama--MacBeth regressions of returns on signals standardized within day; coefficients are in basis points per standard deviation, with Newey--West $t$-statistics (5 lags) in parentheses. The group $\times$ direction mean is the eight-year mean by RavenPack event group and headline direction. Panel B adds to the eight-year category mean a ridge forecast of the return minus its category's training-window mean, estimated on headline embeddings minus their category's training-window mean embedding. No combination weight is estimated; the ridge penalty maximizes the validation rank IC of the combined forecast. Rank IC is the mean daily Spearman correlation with next open-to-open returns, in percent. DM $t$ and JK $z$ test rank-IC and Sharpe-ratio differences from the category benchmark. OOS $R^2$, in basis points, is measured against a zero forecast; its $t$-statistic tests the mean daily difference in summed squared errors from the category benchmark. SR is the annualized Sharpe ratio of equal-weighted (EW) and value-weighted (VW) decile long-short portfolios.
