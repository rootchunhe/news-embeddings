\begin{minipage}{\linewidth}\footnotesize
This table reports mean daily cross-sectional Spearman correlations (rank ICs), in percent, of the historical level and the deviation of the daily ridge forecast with next open-to-open returns for RoBERTa-large and Llama-2 (13B), before and from January 2023, the first calendar year after Llama-2's September 2022 pretraining cutoff. Forecasts are averaged within firm-days with a return to the eligible open and ten subsequent open-to-open returns, on the trading days both encoders share. The historical level is the mean of the firm's monthly average forecast over its news months in the preceding 12 months; the deviation is the remainder. Micro, small, and big stocks are below the NYSE 20th capitalization percentile, between it and the NYSE median, and at or above the median. News intensity is the firm's number of sample headlines in the 12 calendar months before the trading day's month; terciles are formed each day. The relative IC ratio divides Llama-2's ratio of its later to its earlier mean IC by the same ratio for RoBERTa. Brackets report 90\% moving-block bootstrap intervals with 40-day blocks drawn within each period. A relative IC ratio is omitted when Llama-2's earlier mean IC or RoBERTa's later one has a Newey--West $|t|<2$.
