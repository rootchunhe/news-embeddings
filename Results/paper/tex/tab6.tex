\begin{minipage}{\linewidth}\footnotesize
This table decomposes embedding forecasts of next open-to-open returns over 2004--2025. A two-way least-squares projection of the headline embeddings of the up to six years before each test year on category and firm indicators gives category effects ($C$), firm effects ($F$), and the idiosyncratic component ($I$). $F$ is the firm effect for firms with at least ten headlines in the projection window, spread over at least two of its calendar years, and zero for other firms. Applying the forecast's ridge coefficients gives $\hat y-\bar y=\hat y^C+\hat y^F+\hat y^I$, where $\bar y$ is the ridge intercept. Covariance shares divide each component's covariance with returns by the forecast's; variance shares divide its variance by the forecast's. Moments pool firm-days after removing day means. Covariance shares sum to 100; variance shares need not. Standalone columns use each component as a signal. Rank IC is the mean daily Spearman correlation with next open-to-open returns, in percent. SR is the annualized Sharpe ratio of equal-weighted (EW) and value-weighted (VW) decile long-short portfolios.
