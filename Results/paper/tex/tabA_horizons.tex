\begin{minipage}{\linewidth}\footnotesize
This table reports return covariances of the category level and deviation, firm identity, and the idiosyncratic level and deviation, for signal years 2004--2025. Panel A uses the daily ridge forecast, Panel B its headline-weighted monthly average, and Panel C a ridge forecast fitted to monthly average embeddings. Category, firm, and idiosyncratic components come from a two-way category--firm projection of headline embeddings. Historical levels average the category and idiosyncratic components' monthly means over the firm's news months in the preceding 12 months; deviations are the remainders, and firm identity lies entirely in the level. Daily $h=0$ is the nontradable return to the eligible open and $h\ge1$ the $h$-th subsequent open-to-open return; monthly $h$ counts months after the signal month. Cov is the cross-sectional covariance times $10^6$; Share is the percentage of the forecast's covariance, omitted when the latter has $|t|<2$. Newey--West $t$-statistics use 5 daily or 6 monthly lags.
