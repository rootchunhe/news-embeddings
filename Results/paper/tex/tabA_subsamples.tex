This table reports category-mean, embedding, and RavenPack event sentiment score (ESS) forecasts of next open-to-open returns across subsamples, 2004--2025. Micro, small, and big stocks are below the NYSE 20th capitalization percentile, between it and the NYSE median, and at or above the median, on the preceding trading day. Price-derived news comprises stock prices, technical analysis, order imbalances, indexes, and stock picks. Headline-only and full-article are source formats; all forecasts use headlines. Headlines (\%) is the subsample's percentage of all headlines. Panel B reports full-sample differences from the eight-year category mean; DM $t$ and JK $z$ test the rank-IC and Sharpe-ratio differences. Rank IC is the mean daily Spearman correlation with next open-to-open returns, in percent. SR is the annualized Sharpe ratio of equal-weighted (EW) and value-weighted (VW) decile long-short portfolios.
