\caption{Return Regressions on Lagged Deviations and Current Levels}
\label{tab:tab9}
\begin{minipage}{\linewidth}\footnotesize
This table reports monthly Fama--MacBeth regressions of next-month returns on the deviation of month $\tau-k$ and the historical level of month $\tau$, for firms with news in both months, 2004--2025. The historical level is the mean of the firm's monthly average embedding over its news months in the preceding 12 months; the deviation is the remainder. Each component is standardized and residualized on JKP characteristics, converted into a score by its inner product with the maximum Sharpe ratio regression (MSRR) coefficients of its own signal month, and standardized cross-sectionally. Coefficients are percentage returns per standard deviation of the score, with Newey--West $t$-statistics (6 lags) in parentheses. News is assigned by arrival month, and the return month is the $(k+1)$-th month after the deviation's month.
