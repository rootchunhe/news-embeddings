\caption{MSRR Strategy Decomposition with Re-estimated Weights}
\label{tab:tabA_reoptimized}
\begin{minipage}{\linewidth}\footnotesize
This table decomposes monthly maximum Sharpe ratio regression (MSRR) strategy returns with coefficients re-estimated at each horizon, for signal years 2004--2025. Firm-month embeddings are recursively standardized, residualized each month on 132 JKP characteristics, and combined out of sample using MSRR estimated on signal months from 2000. News is assigned by arrival month, and $h$ denotes the $h$-th subsequent return month. Panel A separates the historical level, the mean of the firm's monthly average embedding over its news months in the preceding 12 months, from the deviation; Panel B also separates the category, firm, and idiosyncratic components of the two-way category--firm projection. Each component is residualized like the full embedding and uses the same coefficients, so component returns sum to the total. Ret is the annualized mean in percent after scaling the strategy to 10\% annual volatility at each horizon. Share is the percentage of the total mean return, omitted when the total has $|t|<2$; SR is annualized. Newey--West $t$-statistics use 6 lags.
